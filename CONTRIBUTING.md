# How to contribute

Thanks for taking an interest in this collection. The goal is to keep it useful, careful, and easy to trust.

## Suggest a resource

The easiest way is to [open an issue](https://github.com/coreymcd37/philanthropy/issues/new?template=suggest-a-resource.md) with the "Suggest a resource" template. A short note with the resource name, the URL, and why it belongs here is plenty.

If you would rather send a pull request, that is welcome too. Please keep new material in the same plain, practical tone as the existing notes, and include a working link.

## A few requests

- Please suggest resources that help people give thoughtfully or understand nonprofits better.
- Prefer primary sources and well-known public references.
- If you are sharing your own experience, please keep it factual and modest.
- Please do not submit invented or unverified claims about organizations or people.
