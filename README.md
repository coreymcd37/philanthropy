# Philanthropy

A small, growing collection of notes and resources for giving thoughtfully and supporting nonprofits well.

This repo is a personal place to keep questions worth asking, links I trust for due diligence, and notes I want in one spot as I learn more.

## How to evaluate a nonprofit

A few questions that help when looking at an organization:

- What problem is the organization trying to solve, and how clearly do they explain their approach?
- How do they measure results, and are those results shared in a way that can be checked?
- How much of their funding goes to programs versus administration, and does that split make sense for the kind of work they do?
- Are they open about finances, leadership, and any recent challenges?
- Can their tax-exempt status and basic standing be confirmed through public records or independent evaluators?
- Does this organization fit the cause I care about, and is supporting it a thoughtful way to help?

## Resources

- [Charity Navigator](https://www.charitynavigator.org/) — Independent ratings and financial information on a wide range of nonprofits.
- [GuideStar](https://www.guidestar.org/) — Nonprofit profiles, IRS filings, and organizational data. GuideStar is part of [Candid](https://candid.org/).
- [GiveWell](https://www.givewell.org/) — In-depth research on high-impact giving, with a focus on cost-effectiveness.
- [IRS Charities and Nonprofits](https://www.irs.gov/charities-and-nonprofits) — Official information on tax-exempt organizations, including how to confirm an organization's status.
- [CharityWatch](https://www.charitywatch.org/) — Independent evaluations of nonprofit spending and accountability.
- [Giving What We Can](https://www.givingwhatwecan.org/) — Guidance on effective giving and where donations can do the most good.
