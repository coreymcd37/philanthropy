## How to evaluate a nonprofit

I put a longer, sourced set of notes in [How to evaluate a nonprofit](guides/how-to-evaluate-a-nonprofit.md). It covers confirming an organization's standing, reading its work and finances, using independent evaluators, and deciding whether it is a good fit. There is also a short companion note on [reading a Form 990](guides/reading-a-form-990.md), and a list of [questions to ask before you donate](guides/questions-to-ask-before-you-donate.md).

## Resources

- [Charity Navigator](https://www.charitynavigator.org/) — Independent ratings and financial information on a wide range of nonprofits. See also their [methodology](https://www.charitynavigator.org/about-us/our-methodology/).
- [GuideStar](https://www.guidestar.org/) — Nonprofit profiles, IRS filings, and organizational data. GuideStar is part of [Candid](https://candid.org/).
- [GiveWell](https://www.givewell.org/) — In-depth research on high-impact giving, with a focus on cost-effectiveness.
- [IRS Charities and Nonprofits](https://www.irs.gov/charities-and-nonprofits) — Official information on tax-exempt organizations, including how to confirm an organization's status. See also [About Form 990](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-990) and [Form 990 resources and tools](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/form-990-resources-and-tools).
- [BBB Wise Giving Alliance](https://give.org/) — Charity reviews, [tips for donors](https://give.org/tips-for-donors), and the [Standards for Charity Accountability](https://give.org/bbb-standards-for-charity-accountability).
- [CharityWatch](https://www.charitywatch.org/) — Independent evaluations of nonprofit spending and accountability.
- [Giving What We Can](https://www.givingwhatwecan.org/) — Guidance on effective giving and where donations can do the most good.
